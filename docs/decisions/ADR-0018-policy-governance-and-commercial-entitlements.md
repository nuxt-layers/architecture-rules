# ADR-0018 — Policy Authority, Governance and Commercial Entitlements

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-11-16  
**Review evidence:** [Blueprint PR #39](https://github.com/nuxt-layers/architecture-blueprint-review/pull/39), especially `proposals/policy-and-governance-boundaries.md` and `proposals/commercial-onboarding-and-entitlements.md`. Complements ADR-0017; does not replace ADR-0012/0016.

## Context

'Policy' ambiguously refers to immutable security boundaries, configurable platform/organisation settings, specialist business rules, legal obligations, governing instruments and published policy documents. A notice is not an executable rule; a company administrator cannot weaken anti-takeover floors via Configuration. The host also sells tenant service entitlements, while tenants may sell their own products; those transaction domains must not duplicate or leak commercial authority.

## Decision — policy categories and lifecycle

1. **Identify authority before configurability.** Every rule has an owning authority, declared scope (deployment/tenant/group/person/resource/activity), interpretation/enforcement owner, source/version, effective period, allowed override range, change approval, fail/unknown semantics, retention/audit and withdrawal/retirement path, proportionate to its risk.
2. **Mandatory architectural/security invariants** derive from accepted ADRs and normative standards, are enforced by each affected owning capability and SHALL NOT be tenant-overridden. Configurable **platform operational/security** choices can only strengthen or remain within published, validated security floors.
3. **Organisational governance rules** are authorised through the organisation's legitimate governing authority and may specialise ownership approval, delegation and administrative process within mandatory and applicable legal restrictions. **Business domain policies** remain validated/interpreted/enforced by Booking, Billing, Identity or other respective owner, not by a universal policy service.
4. **Legal/regulatory obligations** and legal holds have jurisdiction, legal-entity, activity and effective-period applicability; `legal-compliance` owns legal duty/constraint context and verification provenance, subject to qualified source review, while `privacy` owns specialist consent, lawful-purpose and retention/rights constraints. Neither may claim universal legal correctness or automatically mutate every domain's records. In unclear/conflicting mandatory obligations, hold unsafe operations for review; never silently fall back to permission.
5. **Governing instruments** (e.g. constitution, articles, council delegation) are evidence of authority, not mere settings. A **published policy document** (Modern Slavery Statement, privacy notice, health/safety policy, cookie notice) has a separate authorship, review and publication lifecycle through Publisher/Content Editor; publication **does not** assert legal applicability, regulatory compliance or technical enforcement. Any corresponding executable control must be mapped to and enforced by its domain owner.
6. **Configuration** owns registered/schema-validated settings, scope/precedence, approved values and provenance, NOT all domain rule semantics or legal applicability. Policies requiring independent complex state/lifecycle may remain owned by their specialist domain; no generic `policies` capability is mandated. Authorisation owns permission decisions, Authentication proof of session/assurance, Workflow orchestration, Audit independent evidence, Logging sanitised diagnostics, Reporting authorised derivatives and Notifications authorised notices.
7. **Protection against policy capture.** Ownership, delegation, assurance, recovery and approval-policy changes are themselves protected operations. A requester cannot immediately weaken and exploit approval rules; preserve independent approval, trusted effective versions, delay/step-up where required, notices and attributable decision/audit evidence. Unavailable, unverified, superseded and not-applicable policies are distinct results.
8. **Policy lifecycle:** define → validate/qualify → approve → schedule → activate → enforce → evidence/review → revise/retire. Owning public contracts specify meaning, version, applicability, bounded configuration, command inputs, outputs, errors, evidence and fail-closed behaviour. This conceptual structure does not impose one universal request object, interpreter or runtime dependency.

## Decision — host commercial services and onboarding

9. **Free personal accounts, paid organisational service activation.** Host MAY offer free self-service person registration; new organisation/tenant provisioning and later feature additions may be subject to a verified commercial agreement. A tenant is not the same as an organisation, application, billing customer or bill-to party. Host operator's business uses its own correctly scoped tenant/commercial records rather than a parallel ad hoc billing engine. Platform-admin authority does not disclose tenant business data.
10. **Commercial owner boundaries.** Product/Pricing own available service/tier and validated quote/discount semantics (specialist promotion ownership remains reviewable); Order owns accepted purchase or amendment; Payment Gateway owns payment/provider evidence; Subscription owns scoped, effective service/seat/storage entitlements and their lifecycle; Billing owns assessment/recurrence/proration/adjustments; Invoice owns issued documents; Accounting owns authoritative postings. Legal counterparties, agreements and bill-to authority require independently evidenced contracts; never infer payer from creator or tenant ancestry.
11. **Four distinct outcomes.** `commercially entitled`, `technically provisioned`, `enabled` and `personally authorised` MUST NOT be conflated. Verified payment or approved credit evidence is a condition under agreed terms, not proof that resources were provisioned. Subscription rights do not imply Identity membership or Authorisation grants; technical enablement is not payment settlement.
12. **Recoverable onboarding.** Application-level onboarding/Workflow coordinates secure, idempotent creation, chosen capability checkbox/tier quotes, coupon reservation/commit, payment verification, subscription, authorised tenant/group provisioning and service activation. Each owning capability reports its independent authoritative state; retries, partial success, unknown provider results, out-of-order events, duplicate webhooks, cancellations, refunds and grace are explicit. Do not expose operator-only Identity migration credentials. Capabilities continue to meet ADR-0012/0016.
13. **Member seats.** Billable licensed workforce/contractor seats are commercial quantities, not Identity memberships; customers, guests, pending invites, cross-group duplicates and non-billable member categories require explicit tariff definitions. Quantity/charge, available seat allowance and actual authorised membership are independently reconciled.
14. **Object storage.** Purchased tenant image/file capacity is an **enforced logical tenant entitlement**, not a requirement to create a new S3 bucket or preallocate provider space. Tenant/group keys and provider isolation remain authoritative under Storage; admission/usage-reservation and concurrent uploads require a defined enforcement boundary before advertising quotas. A usage report alone MUST NOT be represented as quota enforcement. Existing files, retention and legal holds cannot be arbitrarily destroyed on downgrade or payment failure. This does not apply to database, Temporal or infrastructure backups.
15. **Promotions and notices.** Coupons have validated eligibility, version, use limits, expiry, redemption/rollback and commercial authority; an unsuccessful or duplicate payment cannot silently double-redeem. Applicable taxes and consumer/commercial contract requirements must be verified for jurisdiction and circumstance; published legal notices are not sufficient operational proof.

## Required contract / acceptance evidence

- Configured rule cannot undercut domain/platform hard floor; unrecognised, stale or missing critical rule fails safely.
- High-risk actor cannot amend policy and exploit it to become sole tenant owner; evidenced appeal/recovery available.
- Policy document publication, statutory-applicability result and executed control are independently observable and versioned.
- Free user can create authorised organisational request, select independently tiered capabilities, redeem eligible coupon and pay; pending payment never appears as settled/operational.
- One host business invoices many tenants, including separately authorised parent payer; no billing/data cross-scope exposure.
- Payment settles but provisioning fails: no second charge or false enablement; reconciled retry/refund.
- Tenant purchases additional S3 quota; concurrent writes cannot exceed the enforced rule silently; storage reports are not advertised as quotas.
- Adding a workforce seat is charged under defined tariff and granted only to authorised member; guest booking does not create unexpected workforce seat charge.
- Unpaid renewal and over-quota downgrade follow versioned grace/restriction/export/retention rules, not abrupt deletion.

## Compatibility and unresolved matters

Existing Configuration's root contract supports scoped approved setting values, not an executable universal policy engine; existing Storage's root contract says `usage()` reports usage but is **not a limit**. This ADR sets target safeguards, NOT an assertion that implementation exists or an instruction to rewrite completed layers without separate reviewed work.

Open: the party/contract/legal-entity master; applicable statutory-policy adjudication; exact owner of structured governing-instrument lifecycle; user-visible coupon mechanics and promotional owner; seat-counting policy; S3 quota admission/concurrency enforcement owner; entitlement/operational compatibility and refund/grace procedures. Do not introduce duplicate `business`, `tenancy` or `policies` capability repositories solely to implement this ADR.
